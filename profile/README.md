# Copilot Macau 🚌

### Making public transit as simple as taking a taxi.

**Copilot Macau** is an intelligent public transit platform built for Macau.

We believe navigating a city shouldn't require understanding complicated bus routes, memorizing schedules, or guessing when the next bus will arrive.

Our mission is simple: **make public transportation effortless, predictable, and accessible to everyone.**

---

## 🌏 Rethinking Urban Mobility

Public transit systems generate enormous amounts of data, yet passengers are often left with fragmented information and uncertain arrival times.

We're changing that.

Copilot Macau brings together real-time vehicle tracking, intelligent arrival predictions, and contextual city data to create a smarter, more intuitive transit experience.

Instead of asking passengers to understand the transit network, we want the transit network to understand their needs.

## ✨ What We're Building

**🚏 Intelligent Journey Planning**

Go beyond traditional route searches with journey recommendations informed by real-time transit conditions.

**📍 Real-Time Transit Digital Twin**

A live representation of Macau's public transportation network, connecting vehicle locations, routes, and stops in an interactive map.

**⏱️ Predictive Arrival Intelligence**

Data-driven ETA predictions that account for vehicle movements, traffic patterns, and changing operating conditions.

**🌦️ Context-Aware Mobility**

Bringing together weather, holidays, major events, border-crossing conditions, and other urban signals to better understand how the city moves.

**🧠 A Smarter Transit Experience**

Exploring how intelligent systems can make public transportation more proactive, personalized, and effortless.


## 🗺️ Built for Macau. Inspired by Everywhere.

Macau presents a unique urban mobility challenge: a compact, densely connected city with complex bus networks, significant visitor traffic, and dynamic transportation demand.

It's an ideal place to rethink how people interact with public transit.

We're starting with Macau, but the ideas behind Copilot Macau extend far beyond one city.

## 🚀 Our Vision

We envision a future where public transportation feels less like navigating a system and more like having a personal mobility assistant.

One that knows where you want to go, understands what's happening across the city, and helps you get there with confidence.

**Less waiting. Less guessing. More moving.**
